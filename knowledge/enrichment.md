# Enrichment and play
category: enrichment

## Mental stimulation
Puzzle feeders, scent games, short training sessions and rotating toys reduce boredom and destructive behavior. Ten to fifteen minutes of training a day is plenty for most dogs.

## Play for cats
Interactive play with wand toys mimics hunting. End sessions by letting the cat catch the toy and then offering a small meal or treat.

## Social time
Dogs and cats are social. Daily one-on-one attention such as cuddling or play supports mental health. Pets left alone for long stretches may need extra enrichment.
