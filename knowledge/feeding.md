# Feeding
category: feeding

## Dogs: meal schedule
Adult dogs usually do best with two meals a day, roughly 8 to 12 hours apart. Puppies under six months often need three or four smaller meals. Keep meal times consistent so digestion and bathroom routines stay predictable. Breakfast and dinner are the usual meals.

## Cats: meal schedule
Adult cats can be fed two or more small meals a day. Wet food adds water to the diet. Sudden changes in food should be made gradually over about a week to avoid stomach upset.

## Fresh water
All pets need constant access to clean water. Refresh bowls at least once a day and wash them regularly.

## Foods to avoid
Chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol and macadamia nuts are toxic to dogs. Onions and garlic are also harmful to cats. If a pet eats something toxic, call a veterinarian or an animal poison control line right away.
