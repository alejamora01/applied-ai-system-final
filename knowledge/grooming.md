# Grooming
category: grooming

## Brushing
Short-haired pets can be brushed weekly. Long-haired or double-coated pets often need brushing several times a week to prevent mats. Brushing also lets you check for lumps, ticks and skin problems.

## Baths
Most dogs only need a bath every one to three months unless they get dirty. Use a shampoo made for pets. Cats rarely need baths.

## Nails and teeth
Trim nails about every three to four weeks. Brushing teeth several times a week helps prevent dental disease. Use pet toothpaste, never human toothpaste.

## Ears and eyes
Check ears for redness, odor or discharge. Wipe visible dirt gently with a pet-safe cleaner. Persistent redness or discharge needs a vet visit.
