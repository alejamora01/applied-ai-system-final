# Medication and health
category: medication

## Give medication exactly as prescribed
Always follow the dose, timing and duration written by your veterinarian. Never change a dose, double up after a missed dose, or stop early without asking your vet or pharmacist.

## Never give human medicine
Many human medicines, including ibuprofen, acetaminophen (paracetamol) and naproxen, can be dangerous or fatal to pets. Do not give them unless a veterinarian told you to.

## Missed doses
If a dose is missed, contact the veterinarian or read the label instructions. Do not guess.

## Reminders and consistency
Set tasks at the same time every day, and pair medication with a meal when your vet says that is fine. Mark each dose complete so you can see whether one was missed.

## Warning signs: call a vet
Repeated vomiting, trouble breathing, collapse, seizures, bloated hard belly, not eating for more than a day, or blood in stool or urine need prompt veterinary care.
