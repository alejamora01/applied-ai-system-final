# Scheduling tips
category: general

## Prioritize essentials
Feeding, water and medication are the highest priority. Exercise, grooming and enrichment come next. When time is short, defer low priority tasks rather than skipping medication or meals.

## Avoid overlapping tasks
Two tasks for one owner at the same time cannot both happen. Move one task to the next free slot instead of shortening essential care.

## Build routines
Pets thrive on consistent routines. Recurring daily tasks such as breakfast, walks and medication should happen at similar times each day.

## Do not over-schedule
Leave a little buffer in the day for unexpected needs like cleanup, vet calls or a longer walk.
