# Walking and exercise
category: walking

## Dogs: daily exercise
Most adult dogs need at least 30 to 60 minutes of activity per day, split into one or two walks. High-energy breeds may need more. Puppies need shorter, more frequent outings, and senior dogs may prefer gentle, shorter walks.

## Hot weather
On hot days walk early in the morning or in the evening. Test pavement with the back of your hand: if it is too hot for you, it is too hot for paws. Bring water and watch for heavy panting, drooling, or weakness.

## Cold weather
Short-haired and small dogs can get cold quickly. Shorten walks in freezing weather and wipe paws afterward to remove salt and ice melt.

## Cats and other pets
Indoor cats benefit from play sessions instead of walks. Aim for two or three short play sessions per day with a wand toy or similar.
